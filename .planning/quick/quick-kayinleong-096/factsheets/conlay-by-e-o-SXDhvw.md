## Conlay by E&O: top reasons why
1. Designed by architect Kerry Hills, known for completed works of iconic hotels & resorts like Aman Hotel Tokyo, Amanpuri Thailand, and Datai Langkawi Malaysia etc, this development is the epitome of luxury.
2. Fully furnished by the developer, the Conlay designer residence maintains a premium feel and offers an exquisite living experience.
3. Just 220m from Prince Court Medical Centre, it's perfect for retirees seeking top-notch medical services and regular check-ups.
4. Only 280m from MRT Conlay, one stop away from TRX financial district with a 2.2mil sf mall and 10-acre park. Potential for renting out to TRX working elites.
5. Enjoy panoramic views of Golf, TRX, and KL city from three facings of this residence.
6. Developed by E&O, renowned for properties like a 5-star hotel in Penang, St. Mary KLCC, The Mews, and Dual Residency Premium Residences.
7. Just a 3-minute drive along Jalan Conlay to Pavilion KL mall, offering a variety of F&B and international specialty stores.
