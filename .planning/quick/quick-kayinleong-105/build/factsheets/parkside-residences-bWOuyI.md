# Parkside Residences: D2 project facts

Source: D2 project info sheet supplied by the client, 21 November 2026.

## Parkside Residences: project facts
- Area: Setia Federal Hill
- Developer: Setia M S SFH Development Sdn Bhd / JV of SP Setia (Top 10 listed developer Malaysia) + Mitsui Fudosan (Japan's largest developer)
- Land title: Commercial Under HDA. Serviced Residence.
- Tenure: Leasehold till 2116 (developer to attempt renewal for 95–99 years at VP)
- Maintenance fee: Est. RM0.50 psf
- Total units: 693 residential units + 6 retail lots (not for sale)
- Total floors / storeys: 62
- Expected completion / vacant possession: Completion Date: Q1 2030
- Furnishing: Partial Furnished: Kitchen Cabinet, Fridge, Hood & Hob, 2in1 Washer Dryer, Aircond, Digital Lock, Premium bathroom fittings, Wardrone Only in Master Room.
- Car park allocation: 1-2 lots
