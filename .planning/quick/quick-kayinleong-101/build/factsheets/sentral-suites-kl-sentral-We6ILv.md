## Sentral Suites @ KL Sentral: top reasons why
1. Best Transport Hub in ASEAN
Easy access to MRT, LRT, KTM, ERL, and Monorail, linking 10 train lines to 180 stations. Over 200,000 people pass through daily—great for future tenants and buyers.
2. Big City Vibes in 72 Acres
KL Sentral is a complete city with 21 office towers, 5 hotels, 4 residential areas (including Sentral Suites), and a shopping mall all in one place.
3. Second Biggest CBD After KLCC
KL Sentral is home to big companies like Google, Facebook, Allianz, Shell, and more, attracting professionals from around the world.
4. High Rental Demand
With many expats and high-income earners working nearby, monthly rentals range from RM3,000–5,000, giving steady ~5%+ returns.
5. Not Many Small Units Around
Small-sized homes (600–1,000 sq. ft.) are rare in KL Sentral. Older projects like Suasana Sentral Loft don’t offer the same views or facilities as Sentral Suites.
6. Last Residential Project in KL Sentral
This is the last residential plot in the 72-acre KL Sentral township. After this, only two commercial land plots are left.
7. Near Wealthy Neighborhoods
Bangsar, Seputeh, Damansara Heights, Mont Kiara, and PJ are all close by, adding more prestige to the area.
8. Easy to Go Anywhere
Whether by car or train, KL Sentral’s central location makes traveling super convenient.
